01 How it works

Three steps, and a person in the middle.

There is no open signup and no waiting list that exists to collect addresses. Asking is a form and an answer; after the first answer, the rest is yours to take.

Step one

Create your account

Your name, your email and a password of twelve characters or more. A link confirms the address; nothing else is asked.

Step two

Ask for a tool

The first request is read by a person, not by a rule. You get an email when it is approved, and the tool appears as yours.

Step three

Sign in once, everywhere

Once you are in, the next tools open on their own. Each one receives only your identifier, your email and your name.

02 Your security

In one place, shared by every tool.

Second factor

Optional, and yours to remove

Add an authenticator app and signing in asks for a six-digit code besides the password. Take it off whenever you like.

Connected tools

You see what is signed in

Every tool you have signed in to is listed with the date. Sign out of one and its session ends immediately.

Nothing sold

An account, not a profile

The account exists to open the tools. No advertising, no analytics, and no address of yours read in the background.

03 Ready when you are

One door, and it is open.

Create the account, confirm your email and ask for the first tool. Accounts here are approved one by one by a person, which is why the first one takes a little longer than a click.
