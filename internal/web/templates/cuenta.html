Second factor

Passkeys and authenticator apps

With a second factor, signing in asks for more than the password: a passkey (your phone or computer proving it is you with a fingerprint, your face or its PIN) or a six-digit code from an authenticator app. Either protects the account even if the password leaks; you can have both.
